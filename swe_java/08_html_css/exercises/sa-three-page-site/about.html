WHY I LEFT BIKINI BOTTOM

There Is More to Absorb

I love Bikini Bottom. My friends are there, my favorite places are there, and there is always another jellyfish to chase.

But curiosity got the better of me. I wanted to see places I had only heard about, taste food I could not pronounce, meet people completely different from me, and come home with stories worth telling.

Absorb the World is my travel journal for everything that happens between leaving home and finding my way back.

TRAVEL PHILOSOPHY

Rules I Try to Travel By

  • Take Patrick when supervision is not required.
  • Never turn down a good sunset.
