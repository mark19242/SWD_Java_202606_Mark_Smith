Welcome to Moron Mountain

Before they became the Monstars, they were just a group of aliens looking for a way to improve their basketball game. Then things got serious.

The result was a team with size, strength, and absolutely no respect for fair competition.

Warning: Rooting for the Monstars may result in excessive trash talk, questionable sportsmanship, and an uncontrollable desire to challenge cartoon characters to basketball games.

Why We Fan

Sure, the Monstars may have technically been the villains, but let's discuss the basketball.

Elite intimidation.
Incredible size.
Fantastic team colors.
Generational trash talk.

Were their methods questionable? Absolutely. Were they entertaining? Without question.

The Monstars remind us that sometimes the most memorable team is not the one you're supposed to root for.
