Reflection Questions

1. What potential issues could arise from leaving order_total as a string?

Leaving order_total as a string could cause incorrect sorting and make
numeric calculations more difficult. It could also allow invalid
characters to be stored with the monetary values.


2. How does your database's case sensitivity impact query design?

Case sensitivity means text values must use the correct capitalization
when they are compared. Queries may need consistent casing or functions
such as UPPER(), LOWER(), or ILIKE when a case-insensitive search is
needed.


3. What are the risks of relying solely on implicit casting?

Implicit casting may cause unexpected results, conversion errors,
precision loss, or performance problems. Explicit casting makes the
intended data type clearer and more predictable.


4. Why is formatting critical in customer-facing reports?

Formatting makes reports consistent, readable, and professional.
Customer names, dates, and monetary values should be displayed in a
clear format that is easy for the customer to understand.
